The Problem

There are many people, especially youth, who want to learn to experiment with creative tools. These can be tricky to find if you don’t know where to look, and sometimes the tools most readily available aren’t very intuitive for newcomers. As a kid, I would often spend hours on the internet looking for software I could use to exercise my creativity, only to get frustrated when the ones most recommended were too hard for me to learn.

The Solution

These problems I had existed during a time when many people were making their own websites. Some were making blogs to chronicle their journey of finding easy-to-use software for creative projects. I really appreciated their efforts, and now with more useful tools available than ever before, I wanted to make a website showing off a few of my favorites.

The Purpose

Everyone deserves to challenge themselves with effective self-expression. While services may become available to streamline the creative process, the fulfillment of tinkering with available resources to make something of your own is irreplaceable.

Catalogue

Krita

  • Name: Krita
  • Description: A free, open-source digital painting program designed for artists. It excels at illustration, concept art, comics, and texture painting, with strong brush engines and layer tools. Best if you have a tablet!
  • URL: krita.org

Obsidian

  • Name: Obsidian MD
  • Description: A powerful note-taking and knowledge-management app that uses plain-text Markdown files. It focuses on linking ideas through a “knowledge graph” and is highly customizable with plugins.
  • URL: obsidian.md

Audacity

  • Name: Audacity
  • Description: A free, open-source audio editing and recording program. It’s great for basic to intermediate audio work like cutting, cleaning, analyzing, and mixing recordings. Best if you have a microphone!
  • URL: audacityteam.org

OBS (Open Broadcaster Software)

  • Name: OBS
  • Description: Free, open-source software for video recording and livestreaming. It lets you combine sources like webcams, screens, and audio into scenes for streaming or capturing content.
  • URL: obsproject.com

Musescore

  • Name: Musescore
  • Description: A free, open-source music notation program. It allows you to write, edit, and play back sheet music, supporting many instruments and professional engraving features.
  • URL: musescore.org

Twine

  • Name: Twine
  • Description: A free tool for creating interactive, choice-based stories and text adventures. It uses a simple visual interface and requires no coding, though it can be extended with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. A perfect start, if you’ve never made a game before!
  • URL: twinery.org

Godot

  • Name: Godot
  • Description: A free, open-source game engine for 2D and 3D games. It features a node-based scene system, an intuitive editor, and its own scripting language (GDScript), plus support for C#, visual scripting, and more. Great for more advanced game creation!
  • URL: godotengine.org
