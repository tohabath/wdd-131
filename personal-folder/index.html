Welcome!

The Problem

There are many people, especially youth, who want to learn to experiment with creative tools. These can be tricky to find if you don’t know where to look, and sometimes the tools most readily available aren’t very intuitive for newcomers. As a kid, I would often spend hours on the internet looking for software I could use to exercise my creativity, only to get frustrated when the ones most recommended were too hard for me to learn.

The Solution

These problems I had existed during a time when many people were making their own websites. Some were making blogs to chronicle their journey of finding easy-to-use software for creative projects. I really appreciated their efforts, and now with more useful tools available than ever before, I wanted to make a website showing off a few of my favorites.

The Purpose

Everyone deserves to challenge themselves with effective self-expression. While services may become available to streamline the creative process, the fulfillment of tinkering with available resources to make something of your own is irreplaceable.

Jacob Martineau, WDD 131-A1
